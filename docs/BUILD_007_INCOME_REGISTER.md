# Build 007 — Income register

- Adds Income and Payments tabs within Money.
- Adds a spreadsheet-style Income register with direct cell editing.
- Fields: income source, category, amount, frequency and next pay date.
- Calculates monthly income estimates for weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly and annual frequencies; excludes one-off amounts.
- Adds/deletes rows and persists to localStorage (`lp-income`).
- No example income records are seeded.
- This is manual local tracking; no bank connections or payroll integrations.
