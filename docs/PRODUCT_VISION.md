# LifePocket — Product Vision

**Tagline:** Your life, organised in one place.

## Vision
Help people stay on top of the practical commitments that keep everyday life running: payments, recurring subscriptions, tasks, shopping and important documents.

## Problem
People manage everyday responsibilities across bank apps, email, calendars, notes and paper reminders. Important dates and small tasks are easy to miss, while the overall picture of upcoming commitments is difficult to see.

## Target users
- Busy individuals managing household commitments.
- Renters and homeowners tracking housing payments and bills.
- Couples and households who want a shared view of responsibilities (later release).

## Value proposition
LifePocket gives people one calm, private place to record what matters, see what's coming up and take action before deadlines arrive.

## Product principles
1. Useful before clever: core organisation works without AI or bank connections.
2. Private by default: collect the minimum personal information needed.
3. User-controlled: no payments are initiated by LifePocket in the MVP.
4. Clear and calm: show the next useful action, not a wall of data.
5. Accessible everywhere: responsive web experience, installable PWA as a later technical increment.

## MVP success signals
- Users add at least three real commitments in their first session.
- Users return to check upcoming items in a subsequent week.
- Users mark reminders complete and maintain a useful list over time.
- Qualitative feedback confirms that the dashboard reduces the effort of remembering everyday commitments.

## Out of scope for MVP
- Initiating or moving money.
- Open Banking connectivity.
- Financial advice, credit decisions or mortgage recommendations.
- Secure document storage and document processing.
- Automated retailer shopping or purchases.
- Shared household permissions.
