# Product Requirements — LifePocket

## Product vision
LifePocket helps people organise personal commitments, household tasks and everyday financial activity in one calm, useful workspace.

## Build 002 modules
1. Overview dashboard — upcoming commitments, payment summary and tasks.
2. Bills & payments — manually entered payment reminders, recurring frequencies, paid status and payment history.
3. Calendar — visualise payment due dates.
4. Tasks — add, complete and remove personal tasks.
5. Document Wallet index — record document metadata and expiry/renewal planning in later releases.
6. Shopping Manager — categorised shopping lists and completion status.

## Future modules (not implemented in this build)
- Budget vs actual expense tracking
- Income and expense ledger
- Debt and loan planning
- Savings goals
- Household shared spaces
- Full secure document storage
- Scheduled notifications
- AI assistant

## Non-goals for current scope
No bank account management, bank balance aggregation, bank statement imports, transaction synchronisation or Open Banking.

## Quality requirements
- Responsive desktop and mobile layouts
- Clear distinction between demo/local data and connected services
- Local persistence for prototype records
- Accessible labels for key form controls
- No claim of background notifications, cloud storage or bank connectivity
